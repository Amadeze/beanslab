Roastd Studio 0.8.0 - Third Party Notices
==========================================

RoastdDeviceBridge.exe is a separate read-only device-driver process based in
part on the Artisan coffee roasting project's TC4 communication behavior.
It is distributed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later.

The complete corresponding source for the bundled build is maintained in this
distribution's Roastd Studio Artisan fork, including:

  src/artisanlib/roastd_device_bridge.py

Artisan project: https://github.com/artisan-roaster-scope/artisan
License text: resources/device-bridge/LICENSE-AGPL-3.0.txt

The Electron desktop application and AGPL device bridge are separate programs
communicating through a documented newline-delimited JSON protocol over local
standard input/output. The bridge only reads telemetry and never controls the
burner, fan, drum, or other machine actuators.
